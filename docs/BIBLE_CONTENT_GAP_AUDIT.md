Generated during the Classic-style loot pass from the current canon registry and runtime data.

- Saint Veyra Capital
- Hearthmere Fields
- Road Shrine of Little Dawn
- Pilgrim Trial Cryptlet

Playable class:

- Bulwark

- Briarwatch Glen
- Bellscar Coast
- Nocturne Vale

- 11 zone rows

The audit passes, which means the current runtime data is source-governed. It does not mean all source content is playable.

## Missing Or Preview-Only Areas

These areas exist in the compendium/workbooks/source registry but are not playable runtime zones yet:

- Briarwatch Glen: previewed, not playable. Needs route nodes, Thornhouse approach, Greenbell/House Brackenholt social state, local services, enemies, quests, events, and loot tables.
- Bellscar Coast: previewed, not playable. Needs Coastwatch Landing, Cracked Bell Tower, Bell Tower Siege, Roadwarden Tidepost, Oath-Iron Clampworks, Saltbroken lead-in, public events, materials, vendors, and guild contracts.
- Argentwood Cathedral: missing from runtime zone list. Needs holy-corruption route, Synod/Ashen Choir systems, Vows Under Ash event, Chapel of Ashen Vows, cleanse/vow mechanics, and faction state.
- Duskfen Marches: missing from runtime zone list. Needs swamp logistics, Red Choir pressure, Bellgrave Union hooks, transport state, Crypt of the Red Choir, and curse/material systems.
- Veyrholm Highlands: missing from runtime zone list. Needs Hollow Saint pilgrimage chain, Abbey route, highland travel, faction services, and heroic-return hooks.
- Nocturne Vale: previewed, not playable. Needs Red Court etiquette, Guest Right, Blood Debt/Court Favor, Masquerade at Moonrise, Sanguine Court approach, vampire court social stealth, and charm resistance.
- Bloodglass Spires: missing from runtime zone list. Needs endgame approach, Red Court warfront, Castle Veyr preparation, red-crown fragments, guild banners, and raid bridge.
- Mooncrypt Reach: missing from runtime zone list. Needs source pass before runtime planning.

## Missing Dungeons And Instances

Only Pilgrim Trial Cryptlet is playable.

Not playable yet:

- Brackenholt Thornhouse
- Saltbroken Bellworks
- Chapel of Ashen Vows
- Crypt of the Red Choir
- Abbey of the Hollow Saint
- Sanguine Court

The next strongest dungeon candidates from source depth are Brackenholt Thornhouse and Saltbroken Bellworks. Thornhouse has detailed room, boss, wipe recap, material, vendor, and group-finder rows. Bellscar/Saltbroken has strong zone systems, public event support, services, materials, and guild-contract rows.

## Missing System Content

Source-supported but not fully implemented:

- Full class playability for Dawn Priest, Arcanist, Nightblade, Ranger, Oathwarden, Hexbinder, and Wildheart.
- Profession loops for materials, gathering nodes, crafting outputs, vendors, and repair/economy sinks.
- Public events such as Bell Tower Siege, Tidepost Escort, Drowned Banner Rising, Clampworks Surge, Vows Under Ash, Masquerade at Moonrise, and zone-specific repeatables.
- Full source-governed itemization beyond the First Road sample items.
- Dungeon boss tables for the 38 indexed boss rows.
- Rare spawns and rare patrol tables.
- Vendor/reputation inventory unlocks.
- Full faction/world-state consequences.
- Guild projects, Dominion-style contracts, and event-linked guild credit loops.
- Full social scheduling and memory for later-zone NPCs.
- Expanded atlas maps for every source zone, city district, service hub, dungeon room, and boss arena.

## Priority Recommendation

Do not jump straight to every zone.

Recommended order:

1. Finish First Road polish and export proof.
2. Turn the Classic-style loot system into a stable source-law foundation.
3. Expand Saint Veyra/Hearthmere service detail so the starter world feels less thin.
4. Build Brackenholt Thornhouse as the first full 5-player-style dungeon.
5. Build Bellscar Coast and Saltbroken Bellworks as the first major public-event/guild-contract zone.
6. Add the next playable class only after its combat, loot, social, and source rows are ready.
