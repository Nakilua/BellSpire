# BellSpire Goals

BellSpire is a deeply personal, source-governed gothic text MMO project. The target feeling is simple to say and hard to earn: one player should be able to sit down, make a character, and feel like the world is already alive around them.

## Long-Term Vision

- Build a solo-local MMO simulation where NPCs, simulated players, guilds, parties, zone chat, and world events feel active even without a real multiplayer backend.
- Blend text MMO, story game, and DND-DM style narration into one playable local app.
- Keep the world gothic, cathedral-haunted, social, emotional, and mechanically readable.
- Let AI enhance dialogue, mood, and social presence while the app owns state, rewards, rules, and source law.
- Grow BellSpire through source-governed rings: first road, first dungeon, first social layer, then adjacent zones.

## Near-Term Goals

- Make `/login` and character creation feel like the true beginning of the game, not a utility screen.
- Polish the tutorial route from Saint Veyra to Hearthmere Fields to Road Shrine of Little Dawn.
- Make the Pilgrim Trial Cryptlet teach movement, lanes, telegraphs, party pacing, object interaction, and wipe learning clearly.
- Make party chat, guild chat, global chat, and zone chat feel proactive without wasting API budget in the background.
- Improve AI Director quality for NPCs and simulated players while keeping replies source-bound.
- Continue expanding hand-authored topography so maps show terrain, roads, shrines, danger, guild hooks, and locked regions.
- Keep loot strict: every item has rarity, source status, and reward legality.
- Strengthen character identity through origin, vow, class, recap, NPC memory, and social trust.

## Hard Boundaries

- No ghost loot.
- No fake live multiplayer claims.
- No monetization.
- No account system yet. The live realm server (presence and world chat only, per `docs/MMO_EVOLUTION_PLAN.md`) is the first sanctioned backend step; it never owns rewards, combat, or saves.
- No full MMO scope leap before the first playable loop feels good.
- No unsourced canon rename, reward, class change, boss change, or faction change.
- No AI-generated permanent lore without source governance.

## Success Feeling

The first success state is not "huge content." It is:

- The player understands who they are.
- The road feels like it exists beyond the player.
- Chat feels alive.
- NPCs remember enough to matter.
- The first dungeon teaches without feeling sterile.
- Rewards feel earned and source-safe.
- The recap makes the session feel like a real MMO night.
